# Martín Heriberto Pérez Gómez

**Applied AI & GeoAI · Technology Architect · PhD Candidate @ IPN-CIC · Founder @ Majestic Tech**

I build and study AI systems that operate across heterogeneous real-world contexts, with a focus on geospatial intelligence, model generalization, reproducible machine learning, and applied software systems.

## Current research

PhD Candidate in Computer Science at the Centro de Investigación en Computación, Instituto Politécnico Nacional (IPN-CIC).

My doctoral research studies when machine-learning models trained in one geographic context can — and cannot — be reliably transferred to another.

Current areas:

- Geographic transfer learning
- GeoAI and spatio-temporal modelling
- Distribution shift and model generalization
- Reproducible machine-learning pipelines
- Responsible AI systems
